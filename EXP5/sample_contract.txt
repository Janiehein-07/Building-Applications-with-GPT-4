SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is made between ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. and XYZ Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

PARTIES:
ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is the service provider.
XYZ Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is the client.

PURPOSE:
The purpose of this agreement is to provide software development and maintenance services to the client.

OBLIGATIONS:
ABC Technologies shall develop the software according to the requirements provided by XYZ Solutions.
ABC Technologies shall provide technical support and maintenance.
XYZ Solutions shall provide the required project information and cooperate with the service provider.

PAYMENT TERMS:
XYZ Solutions shall pay a total amount of Rs. 2,00,000 for the services.
The payment shall be made in two installments.

DURATION:
This agreement shall remain valid for a period of 12 months from 1 January 2026.

CONFIDENTIALITY:
Both parties shall maintain the confidentiality of business information, technical information and other confidential materials shared during the agreement.

TERMINATION:
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 30 days written notice to the other party.

IMPORTANT DATE:
The agreement begins on 1 January 2026 and ends on 31 December 2026.
